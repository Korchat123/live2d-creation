## Blocking defects

### PLAN-001-01 — Professional anatomy specification is scheduled after anatomy approval

- Severity: **Blocker**
- Evidence: `MULTI_AGENT_PLAN.md` defines the Art Director as owner of measured front-bust proportions and rejection examples, but Wave 3 schedules that work after M0. `FEATURE_TRACKER.md` makes `M1-A Character bible and proportion sheet` depend on `M0 PASS`. Meanwhile M0-A/M0-B may implement and pass Gate B, and `PRODUCT_PLAN.md` requires target proportions to be accepted at the end of M0.
- Impact: the transform graph can be structurally correct yet encode the same monster-like head, neck, shoulder, face, or bust proportions the restart is intended to eliminate. Gate B says such proportions fail but supplies no approved target against which the evaluator can measure them.
- Required remediation:
  1. Move the character bible/proportion contract to PLAN-001 or make it a pre-M0 work package.
  2. Require an independent Gate A pass before M0-A establishes neutral geometry and before M0-B freezes parameter bounds.
  3. Record measurable neutral ratios, allowed presentation ranges, landmark ordering, silhouette rejection rules, and tolerances for head/shoulder, skull/jaw, eye/nose/mouth, neck/collar, bust/torso, and hair envelope.
  4. Make M0 Gate B evidence compare neutral, presets, min/max, and combined extremes to that versioned contract.

### PLAN-001-02 — PLAN-001 delivery state does not follow its own tracking protocol

- Severity: **Major**
- Evidence: `origin/plan/clean-restart-governance` resolves to candidate `4263c82`, but `FEATURE_TRACKER.md` still records PLAN-001 as `building` with an empty candidate SHA and no evaluator evidence. `VERSION_CONTROL.md` sections 3 and 10 require the pushed candidate SHA and review record before completion/evaluation.
- Impact: the first governed work item already has ambiguous source-of-truth state, weakening the exact-SHA review guarantee.
- Required remediation:
  1. Define a non-circular bookkeeping convention: the tracker records the immutable content candidate SHA, while later tracker-only commits do not redefine that candidate.
  2. Update PLAN-001 to `evaluating` or `failed`, record candidate `4263c82`, and link this pushed evaluator report branch/commit.
  3. Submit a new planning candidate only after PLAN-001-01 is corrected; do not amend or rewrite `4263c82`.
